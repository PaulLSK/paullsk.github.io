About the Catchable Trout Availability Index (CTAI)

What is CTAI? A growth-adjusted estimate of how many stocked trout are currently catchable in each waterbody.

  • Growth lags: Species & size specific months to reach catchable size (e.g., Rainbow fingerlings ≈ 24 months).
  • Catchable filter: Only stocking events whose cohorts have reached catchable size are counted.
  • Density metric: Catchable fish per hectare = catchable count ÷ lake area.
  • Species included: Rainbow, Brook, Brown, Splake, Tiger, Arctic Char (Lake Trout excluded).

Known gaps (planned): Natural mortality, predation, angler harvest pressure, and winterkill risk are not yet applied to catchable counts. These will be added as configurable adjustments.
